How we see a brief

A strong brief explains the problem behind the feature.

That distinction creates room to compare approaches before a choice becomes expensive to change. It also gives the people involved a clearer way to discuss scope, assumptions, and what evidence would make the result useful.

Working principles

Four questions
keep scope honest.

Use these to evaluate a digital project before the implementation details take over.

01

What should change?

Describe the user or business task that is difficult today and the difference a solution should make.

02

What already exists?

Current software, data, policies, and team routines shape what can be built or connected.

03

Where are the boundaries?

Clarify access, approvals, platform needs, exceptions, and what should remain under human control.

04

What would count as evidence?

Agree how expected behavior, test findings, and open questions will inform a decision.
